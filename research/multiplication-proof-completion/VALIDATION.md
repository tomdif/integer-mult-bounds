# Validation of the completed written proof package

The new registered suite passed normally and with Python assertions disabled,
in separate fresh bytecode namespaces. It checks 18 amplifying identity
circuits, all 180 contiguous nonempty matrix intervals, 288 executions and
576 bounded normalized-Hadamard calls. The fixtures include three tensor
widths, dyadic and nondyadic diagonal coefficients, coefficient rounding,
arithmetic rounding and adversarial local errors. Each execution checks the
actual supplier input, each local error, every intermediate error envelope
and the final error after undoing the scale. Six counterexamples reject
weakened claims or omitted operations. The complete deterministic RESULT.json
is compared, including the exact rational cost and guard inequalities.

The predecessor fine-prime-transform-assembly registered suite was also
rerun normally and with assertions disabled. It passed its nonstationary
core/face, whole-separator Schur, Fourier/CRT recovery and rejection checks.
Its output is retained in evidence/ASSEMBLY-REPLAY.log. The new suite's output
is evidence/PACKAGE-REPLAY.log; environment and overlap coverage are recorded
alongside them.

The correction adds two coefficient scans per Gaussian group and raises
requested intermediate accuracy by o(p) bits. Native words, moment data,
selected frontier, archives and predecessor packages are unchanged. Their
earlier complete native admission and Lean receipts are retained; no new
native admission or Lean build was performed for this document/code change.

These executions validate finite seams and arithmetic. They do not execute
the asymptotic fast multiplier, check its all-size tape semantics, verify
transcendental estimates in a proof assistant, or provide independent
mathematical review. The new result is the written theorem in PROOF.md,
whose cited lower-level lemmas must be assessed as mathematical proofs.
No hosted CI or human-review result is claimed.
