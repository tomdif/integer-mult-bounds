# Proof dependency and evidence inventory

The endpoint of this package is the written implication in TRANSFER.md:
the specified ordinary interchange and terminal-refit complex-layer theorems
imply exact multiplication at the stated kappa. Those two dependencies have
written constructive proofs in the preceding packages. They are **not**
assumed to follow merely from finite replay. The present work completes an
outer composition candidate; it does not independently re-prove or formally
certify every lower-level theorem in that chain.

## What is changed relative to the retained outer

1. Every fast solve uses a finite length-L core. Nearly full proper cyclic
   principal sections are unnecessary. True periodic boundaries are handled
   by wrapped copies from the original input.
2. Boundary pivots end at whole separator groups. Their leading Schur
   sections are now proved to be Schur complements of the specified finite
   physical prefixes, including the adjacent eliminated interior.
3. Face arithmetic applies the Neumann polynomial to the actual variable
   coefficients, at the explicitly charged p^50 cost. The source's
   translation-invariant convolution theorem is used only for movement.
4. Rounded interior factors and boundary Schur pages are derived from one
   rational core matrix. The original cell-weight rounding is also bounded
   explicitly with polynomial setup precision.
5. The accretive descendant shears use the descendant norm Lambda', and
   the sufficient circuit guard is O(log^2 N+log N log K). This is weaker
   than the retained sharp statement and still satisfies the outer guard.
6. Source transforms are built before CRT. Balanced CRT levels invoke
   exactly d/2 source axes. Conditional phases, global chirps and padded
   layouts have paid sequential generators.
7. Inverse CRT is performed on bounded approximate coefficients before
   integer rounding. Both S normalizations are preserved. The final error
   is bounded by 80*2^(-6b)<1/4.

These are algorithmic changes/joins. Passing a former release checker does
not automatically validate them. Conversely, the existence of historical
conditional language in an immutable predecessor is not a proof that the
new join fails; it identifies the obligation the new text must discharge.

## What the new checks establish

`checks.py` uses exact rational arithmetic for nonnormal, nonstationary
periodic matrices, verifies the full core/first-face polynomial output,
checks both finite inverse comparison residuals, proves exact equality of
whole-group Schur prefixes in its fixtures, and executes the six-stage
inverse on dirty inputs. It also checks exact shifted-Gaussian exponent
identities and the seven-product Strassen formula.

The final multiplication tests execute three normalized transforms in
Q(zeta_S), with the retained frequency units, CRT address maps, both S
scales, inverse CRT, nearest rounding and exact radix reconstruction. Some
cases inject explicit dyadic errors at every completed interface. These
test the recovery theorem on finite inputs; they do not execute the fast
Gaussian compiler or the full physical terminal-refit payload.

`Recovery.lean` proves the error envelope and the resulting nearest-integer
recovery for all real parameters satisfying its explicit bounds. Its final
theorem assumes the approximation error established by the written assembly.
It has no axiom asserting a Fourier algorithm, Gaussian routine or machine
cost. A successful kernel build therefore formalizes the last implication,
not the complete multiplication theorem.

## Open next questions

The main outstanding validation is an independent mathematical audit of B,
C and this composition together, with particular attention to the ordinary
caller's arbitrary complete spectators, native free-operator interpretation,
and simultaneous coefficient-page/row restoration invariants. A feasible
first step is to read the exact primitive statements above against their
construction, then check CORE section 5 and PRECISION sections 3--5.

A full proof-assistant formalization would require tape semantics, the
analytic matrix lemmas and the compiler, far beyond the five recovery
theorems. No executable all-size fast multiplier or practical cutoff is
claimed. A stronger kappa search is not part of this completion task.
